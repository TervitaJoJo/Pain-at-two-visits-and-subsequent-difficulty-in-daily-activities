# Additional external-cohort assessment, 2026-10-30

Status: an added replication motivated after HRS/ELSA findings were known. It is not part of the original study preregistration. This specification is recorded before any new cohort outcome association is fitted. All estimates from the specified cohorts will be retained; ELSA will not be replaced or hidden.

## Objectives and selection
Evaluate CHARLS, specifically requested by the author, and SHARE, selected from available local data because it repeats a general pain question and the five target ADL items. Other local cohorts are assessed at metadata level only, without screening associations. Statistical significance is not a cohort inclusion criterion.

## Cohort-specific measurements
CHARLS: use harmonized release D waves 1--4, with raw health pain variables. Exposure visits 2011 and 2013; outcomes 2015 and 2018. 2011 DA041 yes/no; 2013 WB16: none=0, a little/some/quite a bit/a lot=1. The 2013 question explicitly refers to yesterday. This is a measurement-adapted exploratory replication, not equivalent repeated frequent pain. Never use missing pain-site selections as proof of no pain. Raw IDs are joined through ID_w1 in 2011 and ID thereafter. CES-D10 (0--30, all ten items answered), not CES-D8. Education: harmonized 0/1 -> below upper secondary, 2 -> upper secondary/vocational, 3 -> tertiary. No first-visit function-count sensitivity is planned because walking and stairs differ from the original three functions.

SHARE: release 9 raw waves 5--9. Candidate windows 5/6/7/8 and 6/7/8/9, take earliest eligible exposure pair before viewing later observation availability. PH084 yes=1, no=5. Five ADLs PH049 d1,d3,d4,d5,d6; positive=any 1, negative=all five 0, otherwise missing. Require respondent involvement at both exposure visits (PH054=1 or 2); proxy-only=3 excluded. The respondent-only subset (PH054=1 at both visits) is a prespecified sensitivity. Allow follow-up proxy reports. EURO-D (0--12, all twelve component scores present), not CES-D8. ISCED 1997: 0--2 -> below upper secondary, 3--4 -> upper/postsecondary nontertiary, 5--6 -> tertiary; other codes missing. Arthritis is either rheumatoid arthritis or osteoarthritis/other rheumatism; CVD is heart attack/other heart problem or stroke. Current smoking: present smoking yes/no, never smokers=0. Include country fixed effects. First-visit function count uses walking across room, rising from chair, one flight of stairs. Wave 7 routine health data may be missing by questionnaire routing; follow-up will NOT be silently bridged across an unobserved wave.

## Shared eligibility and follow-up
Age >=50 and doctor-diagnosed diabetes/high blood sugar at first exposure visit. Interview dates known, exposure interval 12--36 months inclusive. Known pain and no difficulty in all five ADLs at both exposure visits. Exclude full proxy exposure interviews: CHARLS raw proxy=0 in 2011 and DB032=1--3 in 2013; SHARE as above. Follow first eligible pair regardless of later availability. Up to two subsequent observed intervals; stop at first ADL event or missing assessment, never reconnect later observations. Actual interval lengths in years. Outcome/exposure not imputed.

## Models and estimation
Primary contrast 11 versus 00. Secondary 01 vs 00, 10 vs 00, 10 vs 11, Holm-adjusted together within cohort and model. Grouped-time complementary log-log GLM, interval fixed effect, log(actual interval years) offset, participant-clustered HC1 variance. Covariates: first-visit age, sex, education, current smoking, arthritis, CVD, depressive score; entry-window fixed effect only if multiple windows. Add SHARE country. No adjustment-variable searches.

Covariate MI: participant-level mice, 50 imputations, 20 iterations, PMM for continuous variables, logistic for binary, polytomous for education. Include exposure, entry, country where relevant, observed event indicator, number of observed intervals, total observed interval time, and first-visit function count where harmonizable. Do not impute outcomes or exposures. Retain subjects without observed follow-up in the imputation input; they contribute no outcome-model intervals. Main models followed by complete-case sensitivity. SHARE additionally first-visit function-adjusted MI and respondent-only exposure-pair sensitivity. CHARLS model remains explicitly exploratory owing to recall mismatch. Record model warnings, rank, convergence and sample/event counts.

If eligibility or metadata are inadequate, report that limitation and do not switch definitions to seek P<0.05. Any necessary correction is documented and the locked specification retained. No manuscript/submission-package edits during this assessment.
